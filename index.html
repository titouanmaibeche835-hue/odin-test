I'm a high school student in France, currently in my final year studying Digital Systems and Information Technology (STI2D, SIN specialization), with a focus that sits at the intersection of software and information systems. I'm largely self-taught when it comes to web development — rather than following a single structured course passively, I've been working through The Odin Project, building real projects from the ground up, including this portfolio itself. My path here wasn't a straight line into computer science. For nine years, I competed in basketball at a serious level, playing point guard — a position that's less about individual skill and more about reading the game, making decisions under pressure, and coordinating with others in real time. When I stepped away from regular competition, I redirected that same discipline and structured thinking toward technology and software. Right now, I've built a foundation in HTML and CSS — semantic structure, Flexbox and Grid layouts, responsive design — and I'm currently deepening my understanding of JavaScript fundamentals: comparisons, conditionals, functions, and how they combine into real logic rather than isolated syntax. I care less about finishing lessons quickly and more about actually consolidating what I learn, which sometimes means slowing down and testing myself before moving forward. Outside of code, I'm drawn to anything that rewards clear, structured thinking — I play chess, I use cognitive training as a kind of mental exercise, and I'm generally more interested in solving a hard problem alone than following a group process. I also have a strong interest in business and negotiation — the strategic, high-stakes side of decision-making, not just the technical side of building things. I tend to learn by trial and error: building something, breaking it, and figuring out why, rather than relying only on tutorials. I prefer working independently, and I'm more focused on building a small number of substantial, well-understood projects than accumulating a long list of shallow ones.
